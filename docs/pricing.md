# Nimbus Pricing Tiers

## Free Plan

The Free plan costs nothing and includes up to 100 notebooks with a 20MB per-note limit. It is free forever, not a trial. Free users can create an unlimited number of notes inside each notebook, attach images up to the per-note size limit, and sync across up to two devices. Notes in the Free plan are searchable by title and full text on all devices, and offline access keeps the most recently opened notes cached locally.

Free users should keep in mind that version history is not included, so deleting a note removes it permanently after the trash window expires. If you rely on long-term revision tracking, upgrading to Pro is the recommended path.

## Pro Plan

Pro costs 30 USD per month (or 288 USD per year) and removes notebook limits. It adds version history, offline search, and priority email support. Annual billing saves 20 percent compared to paying monthly. Pro subscribers can sync across unlimited devices, attach files up to 200MB per note, and export notebooks with formatting fully preserved.

Version history in Pro keeps every save for one year, so you can roll back an accidental edit at any point during that window. Priority support answers within four business hours, and Pro users also get advanced sharing controls such as password-protected public links.

## Team Plan

Team costs 60 USD per user per month. It includes everything in Pro plus shared workspaces, role-based permissions, and an admin console. A 14-day trial is available without a credit card. Shared workspaces let editors, commenters, and viewers collaborate in the same notebook tree, and the admin console shows seat usage, audit events, and export activity for the whole organization.

Team admins can enforce single sign-on through the organization identity provider, revoke sessions remotely, and set retention policies that control how long deleted notes remain recoverable for every member of the workspace.
